# Hero product candidates: gifts-for-her Shopify store (Q4 2026)

Last updated: 2026-10-16

## The plan
- A **gifts-for-her store built around a women's beauty tool with a wow factor**. Drop the gift wording in January and keep running as a beauty tools brand.
- One hero product for the videos, with 2–4 cheap add-ons sold as a "gift set" to raise the order value.
- Wow test: a visual payoff in about 3 seconds, a problem every woman has, not cheap at Target or Ulta, a $40–80 price, landed cost under about 30–45% of the price, under about 600 g, demo-able with just hands, not a knockoff, and room for a product family.

## Current ranking
1. **Automatic curler:** the hero product. Bundle it with the eyelash curler as an "effortless curls" gift set at about $65. The risk is that it's a hot tool, so the sample has to prove quality and safety.
2. **Projection necklace (sterling silver):** the Q4 add-on or second product. Tiny, high margin, a natural gift.
3. **LED mask:** the wildcard. It looks great on video, but a $6.70 cost raises quality concerns and it ships in 5–11 days.

## Christmas order cutoffs (shipping from China, about 2 days of processing)
- Items on 4–7 day lines: last safe order date about **Dec 12**
- Items on 5–9 or 5–11 day lines: last safe order date about **Dec 8–10**
- Show "order by Dec X for Christmas" on the store.

## Supplier notes
- **CJ:** already in the pipeline; free API.
- **TeamDrop / TeemDrop:** free and acts as a private agent. Heavily promoted by sponsored YouTubers, and its Shopify listing shows few installs (about 18 stores per StoreLeads). Use it for quotes and branded packaging, and test with a sample before relying on it.
- **AliExpress:** a backup for US-warehouse stock ("Ships from: United States") and a price check. Order through DSers (free tier). Not the main supplier.
- **FastMoss:** the trial ended Oct 9 (0 credits). The weekly Kalodata and Apify runs cover the TikTok Shop data.
